Welcome

Your warm welcome in the heart of Arbroath

The Portcullis is more than a pub, it's a proper local. Whether you're in for a hearty lunch, a catch-up over a pint, or a big family celebration, you'll find good food, a friendly welcome and a place for every occasion.

When the sun's out, settle in at our al-fresco outdoor seating and watch Arbroath go by. Inside, it's cosy, characterful and always buzzing, from our weekly deals and bingo nights to the games room and function suite.
